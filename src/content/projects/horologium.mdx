Various time-trackers kept dying on me when my internet inevitably dies. A local-first, CLI-based time tracker leveraging [[proj!Pyxis]]' split design and inspiration from event-sourcing to build a sync-resistant time tracker. Index times are 20ms cold / 3ms - 500µs with an existing cache at 130 files. This is due to fingerprinting and bucketing reducing most of the work needed when a cache is already partially built.
