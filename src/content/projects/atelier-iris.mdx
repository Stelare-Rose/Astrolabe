Unifying all my devices, Atelier-Iris is a personal NixOS configuration utilizing flakes to allow me to configure all 3 of my devices (Laptop, PC, and [[proj!Homelab]]) alongside a VM configuration. This has been amazing for reproducible setups, and as such my development environment on my PC, Laptop, or even if I'm SSHing into my Homelab, all feel nearly the same.
